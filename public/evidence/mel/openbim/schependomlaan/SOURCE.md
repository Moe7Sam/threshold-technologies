## Deliberately excluded source material

- Raw IFC design and planned models: Git LFS pointers in the repository; not loaded or shipped to the browser.
- Point-cloud material: not downloaded or shipped due to deployment weight.
- Source BCF ZIP and comparison XLSX: processed locally to derive static web evidence; not shipped publicly.
- Drone imagery/video: repository entry is an external link only; not used.
