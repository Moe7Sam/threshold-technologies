Date: 2026-09-07

This manifest records the founder-supplied production identity set for the Threshold digital ecosystem.

## Identity structure

- Threshold Technologies Holdings — parent group identity.
- MEL — Modern Engineering Lab — engineering, technology and research arm.
- THS Studio — digital-product, presentation and commercialisation-facing studio.

## Shared system

- Charcoal: `#0F0F0F`
- Warm White: `#F9F7F2`

- Golden-ratio / phi proportional logic: `1 : 1.618`

## Controls

1. The three identities remain separate and are never combined into one lockup.
2. The phi grid is construction logic, not decorative marketing artwork.
3. Banners are reference/hero media and do not replace primary logo marks.
4. MEL retains engineering / technology / research positioning.
5. THS Studio website copy follows the current operating architecture: digital products, demonstrations, presentation, commercialisation and customer experience.
6. Do not invent client, partnership, product-maturity or commercial claims.
